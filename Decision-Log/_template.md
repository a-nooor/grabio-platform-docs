# Decision Log — Template

Copy this file for each significant decision: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`

---

## YYYY-MM-DD — Title

**Context:** What problem or question triggered this?

**Decision:** What we chose.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- Option A — why not
- Option B — why not

**Consequences:** What breaks, what to verify, follow-up tasks.

**Repos affected:** grabio.space | ecosystem.git | suba eco sys

**Links:** PR, commit, related [[Backlog/Open]] items

---

## When to log

- Repo boundary or architecture change
- Deploy or security policy change
- UX/product direction lock (e.g. embed vs standalone)
- Anything a future dev would ask "why did we do it this way?"

## When NOT to log

- Routine bug fixes (note in [[Backlog/Done]] only)
- Typo / style changes
